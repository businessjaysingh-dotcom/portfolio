A template should be useful after the click.

A raw Markdown download opened as plain text in the browser. I rebuilt the destination as a worksheet someone could read, edit and take away.

OUTPUTThree working HTML worksheets, with editable fields, text download and browser print.

The problem was at the destination

A download link working technically did not mean the page was useful. The original resource opened as raw text. The revised page puts the question and the fields on screen immediately, with a clear way back to the workbench.

The decision that mattered

Keep the resource readable in the same browser where someone discovers it. Make saving explicit: changes stay in the open tab until the visitor downloads a copy or prints. Nothing needs to be submitted.

  • Before-you-share check: review sources, assumptions and private details

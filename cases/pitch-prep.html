JAY / DEMONSTRATION WORK NOTE

A pitch-prep note starts before the pitch.

Before a useful conversation about a building, someone needs to check the public facts and keep the unanswered questions visible.

INPUTAn anonymised public Toronto property.

OUTPUTA one-page research sample prepared on 5 Nov 2026. No broker commissioned it.

The task

Pull the City’s zoning, height and heritage information into a short preparation note. The work is deciding what those records establish, and what still needs a person to check.

The decision that mattered

A mapped parcel is not automatically the owner’s legal lot. The original note included rough density arithmetic, labelled it a headline number and listed the constraints alongside it. That arithmetic does not establish what can be built. The note keeps the legal-lot question open.

The heritage-district status is also dated. It belongs in a question to confirm, rather than a promise about a site’s potential.

Check the original source systems

  • City of Toronto zoning layer
  • City height-overlay layer
  • City West Queen West heritage-district information

The source systems may have changed since the capture. This sample shows research preparation; it does not establish buyer demand, a valuation or development permission.

# Before-you-share check

Template.

- Does it answer the original question?
- Can each factual claim be traced to a source?
- Is the source still current?
- Are interpretation and unknowns clearly separated?
- Is sample information labelled?
- Have private information and unsupported claims been removed?
- Has someone read it in the recipient’s shoes?
- Is the next step clear?
- A person reviews and sends anything intended for someone else.
